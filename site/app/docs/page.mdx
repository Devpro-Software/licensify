# Overview

## Introduction

Licensify is a versatile software license management suite designed to accommodate a wide range of use cases. It offers a RESTful API, a user-friendly portal UI, and SDKs to facilitate seamless integration into various systems. Licensify empowers developers and organizations to efficiently manage, distribute, and verify digital licenses across diverse platforms. Whether you're overseeing commercial software products or subscription-based services, Licensify provides a robust solution to streamline your license management processes.

![overview](/overview.png)

## Philosophy

Licensify is built on the philosophy of flexibility and adaptability. Rather than enforcing a rigid licensing model, it embraces the diversity of software vendors and their unique needs. Designed to be general-purpose, Licensify provides a foundation that accommodates a wide variety of use cases—from traditional software licensing to usage-based subscription models. While this means that not every specific scenario is covered out of the box, the platform intentionally leaves room for customization and integration. It is especially suited for vendors managing multiple types of software, offering a unified way to track and control license usage without imposing a one-size-fits-all system.
